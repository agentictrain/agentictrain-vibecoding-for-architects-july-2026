# Phase 1 — Browse skills.sh

## What to do

Open [skills.sh](https://skills.sh) and get familiar with the catalog.

1. **Browse the catalog.** What categories exist? What are the most
   popular skills?
2. **Search.** Try these words: "ADR," "architecture review," "API
   spec," "OpenAPI," "codebase," "dashboard," "meeting notes,"
   "decision." See what comes up.
3. **Open a skill page.** Click into a skill. Look at the description,
   publisher, install command, and source files.
4. **Install SkillSpector.** Follow the Day 5 guide, then run
   `skillspector scan --help` to confirm the command works.
5. **Optional extension:** Follow the top-three SkillSpector lab in the
   Day 5 guide after the core route. Clone the source without installing
   it, scan it, inspect every file manually, and record your decision.

Write down three skills that look relevant to your job. You'll search
for more in each exercise.

## Checklist

- [ ] Browsed the catalog
- [ ] Searched for at least three task words
- [ ] Opened at least one skill page and read the source files
- [ ] Installed and tested SkillSpector
- [ ] Bookmarked skills.sh

Optional extension:

- [ ] Scanned and manually inspected the three all-time leaderboard
      leaders and their referenced dependencies
